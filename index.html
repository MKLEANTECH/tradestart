Client integration patterns
TradeStart's clients don't all connect the same way — pick a pattern below to see how each one actually works. Each one's own page includes its flow diagram.
Connect & Pay
Link a bank, then deposit
Lean.connect() links the bank and grants payment permission in one go; Lean.pay() then deposits — both on the same screen.
Data Only
Connect for data, no payments
Lean.connect() with identity, accounts, balance & transactions permissions only — no payment permission requested at all.
AoF Only
Standing consent via Open Finance
Lean.authorizeConsent() sets up a standing Account-on-File consent — no linking step, no card details.
SIP Only
One-off payment, no linking
Lean.checkout() takes a single instant payment — no bank link and no standing consent required.
SIP + AoF Together
Both funding rails, one screen
Offer a standing AoF consent as the recommended option, with one-off SIP as the fallback for a first-time payer.
Connect & Pay
Link your bank
Log in to your bank inside the app. One-time setup, instant — you won't be asked again.
